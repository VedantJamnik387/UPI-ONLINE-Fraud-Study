About this Project

This project is about UPI and online payment fraud awareness. We want to understand common fraud methods faced or observed by shopkeepers and other people.

Some topics included in this project are fake payment screenshots, QR code problems, collect request scams, phishing links and fake customer care calls.

This is an awareness website. It does not connect to bank accounts or check real-time UPI payments.

Common UPI Fraud Methods

1. Fake Payment Screenshot

A person may show an edited or old payment screenshot and say that the payment is completed. Check your own payment app before giving the goods.

2. QR Code Replacement

Someone may put another QR code over the original shop QR code. Always check the receiver name after scanning.

3. Collect Request Scam

A person may send a payment request and say that it is for receiving money. Read the request before entering your UPI PIN.

Common Online Fraud Methods

1. Phishing Link

Fake messages may contain links for KYC updates, rewards or account updates. Do not enter personal details on unknown websites.

2. Fake Customer Care

Some callers pretend to be bank or courier staff and ask for private information. Use official customer care numbers only.

3. Remote Access App

Unknown callers may ask you to install an app or share your mobile screen. Do not give remote access to unknown people.

Basic Safety Tips

  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Check the receiver name before making a UPI payment.
  • Shopkeepers should check the payment in their own app or bank account.
  • Do not click unknown payment or KYC links.
  • Do not install unknown screen-sharing applications.
  • For cyber financial fraud, contact helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
